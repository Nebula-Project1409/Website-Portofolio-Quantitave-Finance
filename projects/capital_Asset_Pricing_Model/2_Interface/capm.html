How to run

pip install yfinance pandas numpy matplotlib seaborn statsmodels openpyxl
python CAPM.py

Needs an internet connection (Yahoo Finance) and a CSV of 3-month government bond yields in the data folder.

What the code does

Downloads daily prices of BBCA, BMRI, INDF and ASII and the market index from Yahoo Finance, converts them to monthly returns, and subtracts a monthly risk-free rate taken from the 3-month government bond yield. It estimates alpha and beta with OLS, computes the market risk premium, and compares the CAPM expected return with the historical return.
