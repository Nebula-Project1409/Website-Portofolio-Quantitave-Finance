What the code does

Downloads daily prices of ASII, BBCA, BMRI and INDF (2021–2025), computes returns and covariance, finds Markowitz maximum-Sharpe weights (max 40% per stock), simulates 1,000 correlated price paths over 100 trading days, then reports the probability of profit, Value at Risk, and the charts below.
