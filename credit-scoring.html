What the code does

Loads borrower data, cleans it, and splits it into training and test sets before any preprocessing. It trains a Logistic Regression (the scorecard model) and a Random Forest, evaluates both with AUC, Gini and KS, converts the probability of default into a 300–850 credit score with Low, Medium and High risk levels, and exports the charts and Excel files.
